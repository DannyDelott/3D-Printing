About this print

All three parts of the sanding plane—body with rear handle, shaped shoe, and locking knob—arranged on one P1S build plate. The shoe follows the curve cut by the Magnate 5869 router bit. After printing, slide the shoe into the body and tighten the knob by hand.

  • Matching router bit: Magnate 5869
  • Original tote: Stanley No. 5 handle by vikingth0r on MakerWorld
