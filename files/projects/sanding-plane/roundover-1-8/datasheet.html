Print and fit

Print rail-cap-down with the groove upward, without supports. Apply an approximately 5 mm wide strip of thin PSA sandpaper to the curved seat only. Leave both short shoulders bare; the material beyond them is cut back to clear the board faces. Hold the plane at 45° to the two board faces and move along the edge.

Print the coupon first. It uses the same cross-section and fits the existing 70 mm coupon carrier and tapered knob. The full shoe uses the existing 186 mm carrier and tapered knob.
