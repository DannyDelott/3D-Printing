Fingernail-profile caul

About this print

A shaped clamping block for gluing veneer onto the curved face of a board cut with the Magnate 5869 fingernail router bit. Place a 2×4 along its flat back to spread clamp pressure over the veneer. Cover the contact face with packing tape to keep glue from sticking to it.

  • Matching router bit: Magnate 5869
