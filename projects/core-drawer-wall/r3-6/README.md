# Cardboard core drawer wall · R3.6

[Clickable 3–2–3 assembly](datasheet.html) · [First cell](models/starter-r3-6.3mf) · [Core fit sample](models/fit-r3-6.3mf)

Use nominal 83 mm ID × 88 mm OD × 60 mm cardboard cores on a continuous shelf. The shelf carries the weight. The only loose hardware in the example is two #8 × ¾″ pan-head wood screws into the plywood backboard. The rear clip and rail print as one piece. Two small shoulders on the rail latch over the front socket.

## Outer pockets

| | R3.5 | R3.6 |

| Body width | 22.00 mm | 36.55 mm |
| Volume to rim, each | 88.6 mL | 146.3 mL |

The old design held the whole bin outside the tie and diagonal braces. The upright and rail now sit along the inside edge, and the bin corners follow the adjacent cores. The body has 0.40 mm window clearance per side. The centerline of row two leaves 8.0 mm between the inner wall of the bin and the neighboring core. Both example pockets are fully seated. ## Print and assemble

1. Test the [single foot pair](models/fit-r3-6.3mf) on an actual core. Press the rear jaw over the rim with the rail running along the outside. Fit the front jaw to the opposite rim and press it over the split tip until both shoulders clear the face. Pull on the front clip to check that it stays captured. To remove it, pinch the fork tips together and slide the front off. Nominal socket clearance is 0.25 mm per side. The snap is sized for a 60 mm core.
2. Build from a level shelf. Install the [outer feet](models/dry-ends-r3-6.3mf), [shared bases](models/base-mount-r3-6.3mf) and [upper junctions](models/upper-mount-r3-6.3mf) as linked front/rear pairs. Install the [left](models/left-pocket-r3-6.3mf) and [right](models/right-pocket-r3-6.3mf) side frames before adding the outside top cores. Their uprights carry those cores even when a pocket is removed. The [triangle kit](models/triangle-pocket-r3-6.3mf) fits an inverted three-core gap.
3. At a chosen middle anchor, pilot the plywood and drive the wood screw through the rear junction before seating the front sockets and cores around it. For the top anchor, install the [top set](models/top-tier-r3-6.3mf) or add [top clips](models/top-clips-r3-6.3mf) to an existing upper junction. The screw enters the backboard through the rear tab above the core. The [eight-row elevation](../r3-2/layout.svg) illustrates anchor locations only.
4. The 3–2–3 example takes eight R3.5 main drawers, one set of outside feet, two shared base pairs, two upper pairs, one complete top set, two side pocket kits, three triangle kits, and two wood screws. One core is shown empty to expose its seats. Choose [open](../r3-5/models/open-r3-5.3mf), [left/right](../r3-5/models/left-right-r3-5.3mf), or [front/back](../r3-5/models/front-back-r3-5.3mf) drawers.

Slice estimates below use P1S PLA, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 15% gyroid. Print with the rear faces on the bed and the rails pointing up. Supports and brims are disabled. [CAD measurements](checks.json) and [Bambu slice results](slice-checks.json). Rim fit, snap retention over repeated use, and loaded stack strength await a physical test.
