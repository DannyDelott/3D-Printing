Tracing and shaping
  1. Rails: butt the two halves at the door centerline, hold their straight back edges against one straightedge, and tape across the joint. Together they cover 454.025 mm / 17⅞″. Mark both long narrow edges of the rectangular rail blank. Bandsaw outside the curve, then plane and scrape to the lines. The same pair fits both rails.
  2. Stiles: tape the matching L or R pattern onto each rectangular blank’s end grain, aligning its straight edge with the flat back and its side with the correct outer door edge. Trace the curved edge, then plane down to the lines. At the opposite end, flip the template while keeping inner and outer edges on the same physical sides of the blank.
  3. Panels: use the CORE patterns on the bare core before applying veneer. These allow 0.6 mm maple on the curved face and 0.6 mm on the flat back. FACE patterns check the finished veneered envelope. Do not plane the full crown into the thin veneer. Glue-line thickness is not included.
  4. These are cross-sections for rectangular blanks. Shape the crown first; cut the asymmetric end outlines, groove tongues and shiplaps separately from the flat back datum. The templates extend the field curve through material later removed at those edges. They do not describe the final grooved or lapped end-grain silhouette.
  5. Keep the pencil upright against the template edge. Measure the printed widths before marking good stock and remove any first-layer lip without rounding the profile. Print placement assumes at least a 228 × 40 mm usable area for each rail half; your printer model is unconfirmed.
Dimensions and construction notes

The arc comes from the saved R7 door: 454.025 mm wide, 19.05 mm crown, 1362.141 mm radius. Rails finish at 19.05 mm at the outer edges and 38.10 mm at the center. Stiles retain their 3.175 mm front setback. The panels follow the same bow while retaining their recessed face position.

Panel blank widths retain the R7 shiplaps, so the side blanks are different widths: L 97.5125 mm, C 158.1000 mm, R 104.2125 mm. The visible center bay remains 6″. These widths are measured from the saved CAD geometry, including hidden edge stock.

The 6.10 mm finished tongue thickness remains an assumed fit inside the 6.35 mm groove. With 0.6 mm veneer on each side, the nominal core at a flat tongue is 4.90 mm. The center core reaches 23.95 mm before veneering. Core material, bonding, bending and edge treatment need a sample; this set validates the curve rather than the complete panel construction.

The requested 1/64″ shadow-line cut is 0.396875 mm deep, leaving only 0.203125 mm of a nominal 0.6 mm face veneer. Test the depth on an offcut; that channel is a later operation and is not cut into these tracing edges.
