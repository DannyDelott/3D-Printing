Groove section

Grooves and through-dovetails are cut into the CAD solids. Knife hinges are mortised. The round knob projects 18 mm from the stile, beyond the 6″ case-and-door depth. Knife leaves and clearance mortises are custom geometry, not a verified off-the-shelf installation. Screw design, hinge loading, frame joinery and final machining allowances remain open.
