About this print

The front grip also acts as the sanding plane’s locking screw. Turn it by hand to seat its tapered tip in the shoe and hold the shoe in place. Loosen it about one turn to slide the shoe out without removing the knob from the body.

  • Matching router bit: Magnate 5869
