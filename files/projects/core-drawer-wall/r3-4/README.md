# Cardboard core drawer wall · R3.4

[Mock assembly, print orientation and mounting diagram](datasheet.html). The clickable mock assembly has three bottom cores, two in the middle and three on top. Reveal connectors hides the drawers and makes the cardboard translucent; orbit to the back to see where the plywood screws go. One drawer per nominal **83 ID × 88 OD × 60 mm** bare cardboard core. The bin, recessed finger cup and full circular front are one solid, including an optional fixed divider. No screws, glue or hinges in any drawer. The Ø86.8 mm face overlaps the nominal bore by 1.9 mm around its whole circumference. It covers the lower opening; the 3.6 mm clearance between adjacent fronts remains for withdrawal.

**First print.** Try the [3.57 g core fit sample](../r3/models/fit-r3.3mf) if your tube is oval or its size is uncertain. Print the starter at 100%, leaving the drawer tilted as supplied. The lower bevel provides a 702 mm² first layer with the drawer floor inclined 38°. The face has a closed-back 34 × 14 mm rounded finger opening, 9 mm deep, with a 3.2 mm lip behind its upper edge. The flat writing face is 14 mm farther forward than the R3.3 face, but extends only 0.6 mm beyond the old pull tip. The additional space becomes storage. PLA, P1S, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layers, three walls, 15% gyroid, supports and brim off. Do not use auto-orient. Two ordinary 2.5 × 200 mm ties connect the feet front to rear; slide the completed drawer in level. Drawer hardware: none. Against the 82.4 g / 2 h 12 min cardboard-can baseline the complete starter saves 24.74 g and 16 minutes (slicer estimates).

**Add a bottom cell.** Reuse the outside feet at the new end; add a [shared base](../r3/models/base-mount-r3.3mf) and one tie at the new seam. Use one drawer of your choice.
**Add an upper cell.** Nest it between two cores underneath; tie one [upper connector pair](../r3/models/upper-mount-r3.3mf) front to rear. At selected joints substitute the [middle mount](../r3-2/models/middle-mount-r3-2.3mf). For a new anchored top cell, print the [complete top set](../r3-2/models/top-tier-r3-2.3mf); for an existing top cell use [top clips only](../r3-2/models/top-clips-r3-2.3mf).
**3–2–3 layout.** Three bottom cores, two in row two and three in row three. Fit the [triangle spacer](models/triangle-spacer-r3-4.3mf) in each downward-pointing three-core void: one above the middle bottom core and two between the middle and top rows. Each plate prints a front and rear pair (5.59 g / 17 min) and takes one tie. These optional dust fillers are in addition to the ordinary junctions.

Print one [left side support](models/edge-left-r3-4.3mf) and one [right side support](models/edge-right-r3-4.3mf). Each file contains a front cover and rear frame (9.49 g / 28 min). Seat both in the half-gap beside row two, joining the lower outside core, adjacent middle core and top outside core. Piloted by the lower rim and intermediate rim, the uprights cradle the third-row rim and guide its drawer. The closed front faces outward and the open frame goes behind; pass a 2.5 × 200 mm tie through the slots, with its head at the front. Fit the side support before loading that outside top drawer; the small triangle spacer cannot carry this position.

For the example: 8 drawers and 8 bare cores, 1 outside-feet set, 2 shared base pairs, 1 ordinary upper pair, 1 middle wall mount, 1 top mount set (including its upper junction), and the two handed side-support pairs. Add 3 optional triangle-spacer pairs to close all three voids. With all spacers installed: 11 cable ties and 2 wall-anchor bolt/screw sets. The eight-row stair-step example linked below is a different arrangement.

**At the wall.** Use one M3 × 70 mm flat-head machine screw, washer and locknut plus one #8 × ¾″ wood screw per anchored station. The bank rests on a continuous shelf. [Hardware assembly and eight-row parts list](../r3-2/README.md#connector-plates-and-hardware) · [eight-row layout](../r3-2/layout.svg) · [mount detail](../r3-2/mount.svg). Use the R3.4 drawers in place of the old two-piece ones; only the wall mounts take screws. The layout illustrates positions, not an established eight-row load rating. Support drawers near full extension.

**Digital checks.** Drawer 3MFs each contain one closed volume; every sampled 0.2 mm layer region intersects material below it. Projected centers of mass fall inside the first-layer footprint for each drawer. The complete assembly was checked for spacer interference against the cores, ties, other connectors and drawer travel. The starter, all three drawers, both side-support plates and triangle-spacer plate sliced without supports, brim or slicer warnings. Fit and strength have not been physically tested.
