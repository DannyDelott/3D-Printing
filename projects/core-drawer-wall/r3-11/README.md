## Print and attach the drawer front

1. Print the matched 3MF as arranged. The tray sits on its floor, with a full, flat 1.65 mm rear wall. The separate 86.8 mm front prints label-side down. The open bin and both straight-divider variants take the same front.
2. Assemble outside the cardboard core. Hold the finger cup at the top and the face about 24 mm higher than the tray. Position the two open-ended sockets above the tray rails. Slide the front straight down until it stops and the center tab clicks. The sides of the dovetails carry the pull; the spring prevents the front from sliding back up.
3. Dry-fit the small sample first, using the same filament and print orientation. Check that it seats by hand, resists a straight pull and stays down. If you want a permanent joint, use a small dab of adhesive suitable for your filament on the two flat pads below the rails. Keep glue away from the spring and dovetail channels. No screw or adhesive is required by the joint design.

The joint is outside the storage cavity. The tray has no rear ribs or angled print facet; a notch in the front wall clears the blind cup. A short hood covers the projecting nose and the round face closes the gap below the tray floor. The enlarged front stays at least 0.8 mm ahead of the front rim clips.

Use nominal 83 mm ID × 88 mm OD × 60 mm cardboard cores on a continuous shelf against ¾″ plywood. The [enclosed side housings](../r3-10/README.md), [three pencil cubbies](../r3-9/cubbies.svg) and four backboard screws stay in the 3–2–3 assembly. The first cell contains an open tray, one face and four short rim feet.

[CAD checks](checks.json) · [Slice results](slice-checks.json). All six plates sliced on a P1S, PLA, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layers, three walls, 15% gyroid, without supports or brim. Joint engagement, assembly motion and drawer withdrawal were checked digitally. Snap feel, pull strength and long-term wear have not been physically tested.
