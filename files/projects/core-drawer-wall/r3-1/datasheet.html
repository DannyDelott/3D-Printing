What the flats do

The lower bearing surfaces locate the drawer on the base. Their slope builds without support. Keeping the same flat floor but removing every diagonal cut would gain 6.7 mL; just 0.5 mL is in the two upper corners. The circular upper face can be taller and rounder than the open tub behind it.

Dust and cleaning

The face shields the open top and sides. The short bottom flat remains at bed level, so this is not a gasket or a sealed bin. Rounded floor, side and end transitions make the inside easier to wipe. The pull slopes down toward its nose instead of forming a horizontal shelf. Pull drawers out to clean; exposed valleys between the top-row cores remain.

Print and fit

Print as supplied, floor down. The recorded P1S slice uses 0.20 mm layers, three walls, 15% gyroid infill, supports off and no brim. The nominal core is 83 mm ID × 88 mm OD × 60 mm. Drawer travel and neighboring face clearance were checked in CAD; physical fit and dust exclusion haven’t been tested.
