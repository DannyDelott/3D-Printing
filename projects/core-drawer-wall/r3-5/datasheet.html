Print orientation

Print the completely flat circular face directly on the bed. Its recessed finger cup is closed behind. The hidden rear wall is pitched so it closes without supports. Turn the bin level to install. The Ø86.8 mm face overlaps the Ø83 mm bore by 1.9 mm at the bottom as well as the sides.

Build the 3–2–3 bank

Use the gaps: The left and right outer pockets hold 88.6 mL apiece. Three triangle pockets hold 13.8 mL apiece. Each kit prints a pull-out bin plus its fixed front/rear frames. The outside frames carry the top cores.

Bottom row: Move the outside feet outward and add one shared base at each new seam. Use the fit print to try the rim and drawer clearance.

Upper rows: Interior upper cores rest between two below; use one upper connector pair. Tie the front and rear jaws together.

Backboard: Use a rear wood screw in the same tied upper pair at a middle anchor. The top clips also use a cable tie; only the backboard gets screws. Use the anchored top set for a new top cell, or top clips only to retrofit. Screw detail.
