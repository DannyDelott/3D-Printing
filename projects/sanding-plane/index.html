About this print

A hand sanding plane with interchangeable profile shoes. The rear handle gives you a grip while the shoe supports the sandpaper against the routed surface. Slide the selected shoe into the body and hand-tighten the front knob; loosen the knob to swap shoes.

  • Original tote: Stanley No. 5 handle by vikingth0r on MakerWorld
