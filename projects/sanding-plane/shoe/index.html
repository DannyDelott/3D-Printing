About this print

The removable sanding surface for the plane. Its curve follows the 1½-inch radius of the Magnate 5869 router bit across a 1¼-inch-wide board face. Add sandpaper to the shaped face, slide the shoe into the body, and tighten the matching tapered knob.

  • Matching router bit: Magnate 5869
  • Original tote: Stanley No. 5 handle by vikingth0r on MakerWorld
