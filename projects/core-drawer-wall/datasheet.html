Build a row

  1. Try the fit plate. Slip its glide onto the bottom rim. Run the short drawer sample through the bore, flat edge down. Test its seam clip on two core ends side by side. Loose fits can be bonded; don’t force a tight clip into the cardboard.
  2. Start on a shelf. Put one flat foot at each end of every bottom core, both centered at six o’clock. Add a clip at the front and rear of each touching pair. Align the core ends and check that the drawer slides freely before bonding the clip and shoe jaws to the cardboard with small drops of CA glue.
  3. Add an upper cell. Nest its core between two below; use two small glides in place of the feet. Join it to each lower neighbor and to the next core in its row. The upper plate covers all three new seams. Print one drawer per core.

Attach to ¾″ plywood

Use the wall tab and front guard at selected exposed top cores. Slip the guard onto the top front rim. Feed a 4.8 × 200 mm cable tie along the top inside the core and back over the outside, through both rear tab slots. Seat the return in the recess against the plywood; snug the tie with its head on top outside the tube. Fasten the eye with a #8 × ¾″ pan-head wood screw (about 14 mm of penetration).

Anchor separated points near the upper corners. The shelf carries the weight; the ties restrain tipping. Physical fit, safe load and maximum spacing are untested. The drawer lifts out without a stop; support it as it clears the front glide.
