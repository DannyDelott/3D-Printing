## Printing

Each 3MF contains one basket. Open in Bambu Studio with its saved P1S settings: 0.4 mm nozzle, PLA, 0.20 mm layers, 2 walls, 15% infill, supports enabled. Saved settings were retained in all three exports. All three files successfully sliced with no reported warnings. Filament estimates include the enabled supports. Print two copies of the wide basket, three copies of the standard basket, or one narrow replacement to use with the two originals.

3MFs were exported from the generated MakerWorld configurations. STLs are derived from the exact 3MF build geometry. Parameter values are saved in parameters.json for future regeneration.
