About this print

A 70 mm test shoe for the 1/8-inch roundover profile. Try it on a routed scrap with your sandpaper before printing the full shoe. It fits the existing 70 mm coupon carrier and tapered knob. Line the groove and both guide faces with thin sandpaper, including the assumed 0.30 mm thickness allowance. Hold the plane at 45° to the adjoining board faces and sand along the edge. Print rail-cap-down, groove-up, without supports.

  • Matching router bit: TOTOWOOD 1/8-inch roundover
