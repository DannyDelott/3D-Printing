# Cabinet door templates

Terms used to describe the cabinet door and the tools used to shape it.

**Door design**: The assembled door’s dimensions, curve, and matching rail and stile profiles.

**Rail**: A horizontal wooden frame member at the top or bottom of the door.

**Stile**: A vertical wooden frame member between the rails; left and right share a profile rotated 180°.

**Routing template**: A guide whose edge transfers a frame profile to wood using a matching router bearing and cutter. Template thickness is independent of wood thickness.

**Template revision**: A specific combination of profile, template thickness, openings, and split-joint geometry. Revisions are local to a design and frame member.

**Split joint**: The dovetail joining printed sections of a routing template. It is separate from the joinery used to assemble the wooden door.

**Fit coupon**: Small pieces extracted from a particular joint revision to test its mating fit before printing the full template.

**Print layout**: Placement and orientation of template pieces on a printer’s build plate, with the associated slicer settings.

**Physical fit approval**: Confirmation from an actual printed fit check; CAD checks and successful slicing alone do not establish it.
