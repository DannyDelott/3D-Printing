About this print

A 70 mm roundover profile coupon for checking the 1/8-inch radius and cushion fit on a routed scrap. The preview and downloads contain only the profile. Reuse the shared 70 mm coupon carrier and tapered coupon knob; use the Assembly fit coupon to test the complete connection. R3 allows one layer of 1/8-inch POWERTEC 71014 cushion plus 0.30 mm for paper and adhesive. Keep the cushion and abrasive in the curved seat and leave the relieved shoulders bare.

  • Matching router bit: TOTOWOOD 1/8-inch roundover
  • Cushion: POWERTEC 71014 · 1/8 in mat
