# R3.1 round-front drawer

A one-piece drawer option for the fixed R3 wall. The unchanged R3 upper junctions, shared bases, nominal 83 × 88 × 60 mm cores and 43.8 mm mouth all carry over. [Design sheet and interactive model](datasheet.html) · [Drawer 3MF](models/dust-shield-r3-1.3mf).

The lower 45-degree facets support the print on its floor and locate it on the existing base. At the same flat floor and rim height, removing every diagonal from the interior would increase the R3 cavity from 242.3 to 249.0 mL. The two upper cuts account for only 0.5 mL. A round bottom without any flat floor would hold roughly 255 mL at the same rim height; it wouldn't sit or print like the original.

R3.1 keeps these bearing surfaces. Its taller, nearly circular front covers the upper mouth and sides of the core when closed. The two lower front corners follow a 45-degree tangent from the flat floor into the circle. The top of the integral pull slopes toward its nose. Inside the drawer, 2.4–2.5 mm blends ease the end-wall, side and floor transitions. CAD cavity: 241.8 mL, versus 242.3 mL for R3. This is a dust shield, not a seal. There is a 6 mm opening below the floor and the top row's exposed cardboard valleys remain.

Print the supplied orientation (floor down), with no supports and no brim. The P1S / PLA slice at 0.20 mm, 3 walls and 15% gyroid estimates 43.46 g and 73 minutes; the R3 drawer is 40.49 g and 56 minutes. No slicer warnings. The CAD checks cover full straight withdrawal, closed adjacent faces and all six neighbor directions. Physical fit, dust performance and finish are untested. Source imports the R3 base geometry; run source/model.py and source/drawing.py using the CAD Python.
